Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026 Last Updated: August 1, 2026

At nodedCode Studio, we respect your personal privacy and are fully committed to protecting any information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

When you submit project inquiries, contact forms, or communication requests through our platform, we may collect the following information:

Your name

Your email address

• Project details or technical requirements you choose to share

We do not require account registration to browse this website. We collect only what is reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and facilitate professional communication.

2. How We Use Your Information

Any information you provide through our contact or inquiry forms is used solely for the following purposes:

We maintain a strict policy against the sale, rental, or unauthorized distribution of your personal data to third parties. Your submitted information is kept strictly confidential and is not shared with external parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

Information submitted through contact or inquiry forms is retained only for as long as is necessary to process your inquiry and fulfil the stated purpose of collection. We do not store personal data beyond operational necessity, and we do not maintain long-term databases of personal information submitted through our general website forms.
